## How to use

1. Open any post with media.
2. Tap Share, then FREE Media Downloader (you can rename this from the shortcuts app so it doesn't make your Share Sheet look ugly).
3. Wait a bit. The file will then be on your device ✅.

You can copy a URL and run it from Shortcuts the same way

## Apps

❕ Install **a-Shell mini** or **a-Shell** before you share a YouTube or Instagram link - either one works, the actions are the same.
