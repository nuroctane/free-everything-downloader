Share a link. Save the file. No key. No paywall. No checkout.

Too many downloaders advertise a tap and then bill you for the tap. The share sheet is already on your phone. The save is where they collect.

They wait until you already wanted the file, then they ask for a key, a subscription, a speech about supporting the developer. Convenience was the pitch. They sold it back to you at the register.

Free EVERYTHING Downloader keeps the pitch. Share a post, or paste a URL. Video and photos land in Photos. Audio lands in Files. TikTok comes off the watermark when the source lets it. YouTube always goes through yt-dlp. Instagram is the original file, no watermark overlay. No account. No key file. No Pro screen. A dead link dies in place. Nothing opens a checkout.

If a post sits behind a sign-in, the shortcut opens it in Safari or the native app. Sign in there. Share the post again.

If you have been paying rent on a button that was already on your phone, stop.

## How to use

1. Open the post.
2. Tap Share, then Free EVERYTHING Downloader.
3. Wait. The file is on your device.

You can copy a URL and run it from Shortcuts the same way.

## Apps

Install **a-Shell mini** from the App Store before you share a YouTube or Instagram link. That is the app this shortcut calls. Full a-Shell is a different app and is not used.

Safari is already on the phone. YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and X are optional; use them when you already sign in there.

## One-time permissions

The first Photos save asks. The first Files save asks. The first a-Shell mini run asks. The first yt-dlp install inside a-Shell mini may ask for network. Approve each once.

## Sign-in walls

Private and deleted pages fail when the site refuses the file. A login wall opens in Safari or the app. Sign in. Share the post again. If you keep a Netscape `cookies.txt` in a-Shell mini's Documents folder, yt-dlp will use it.
